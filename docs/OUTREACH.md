## Track A — GPU cloud operators (supply side)
*Profile: Nebius / Verda-class European and GCC operators with idle or
under-contracted capacity (esp. H200 / MI300X / Ascend fleets).*

**Why they care**
- **Reach demand beyond the rolodex.** Structured buyer requests arrive as
  normalized specs (e.g. 256× H200 · 24 mo), not forwarded PDF chains.
- **Comparable pricing.** Quotes normalized to $/accel-hr — they compete on
  merit and evidence, not on who answered email fastest.
- **Evidence becomes an asset.** Benchmarks, facility and trade-control
  documentation feed an eligibility record once — reused across every buyer.
- **No disintermediation, no custody.** Contracts and payment stay
  counterparty-to-counterparty; the venue never holds funds or the customer.
- **Curation, not gatekeeping.** Listings pass an operator review gate
  (pending-by-default, audited approvals) — quality signaling buyers trust.
